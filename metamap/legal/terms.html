Terms and Conditions — MetaMAP Drill Alerts (SMS)

Last updated: September 18, 2026

These terms govern the MetaMAP Drill Alerts text-message program operated by Boss.Technology (“we”, “us”). MetaMAP is an emergency-operations platform used by organizations to rehearse their response to emergencies.

1. Program description

MetaMAP Drill Alerts sends text messages to staff and coordinators who have agreed to take part in emergency drills. During a drill you may receive:

  • a message announcing the simulated emergency — every message is labelled SIMULACRO / DRILL and states that it is not a real emergency;
  • a welfare check asking you to reply SI (or Y) if you are safe, or NO (or N) if you need help;
  • one reminder if you have not replied; and
  • a confirmation once your reply is recorded.

Messages are sent only during drills. They contain no marketing or promotional content and no links.

2. Consent

You receive messages only after you have given written consent to your organization’s emergency coordinator using the MetaMAP Drill Alerts consent form. Your number is then added to an approved drill list. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or of employment.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies with the drill schedule, with up to 4 messages per person per drill.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for these messages; your mobile carrier’s standard rates apply to messages you send and receive.

5. How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You can also ask your emergency coordinator to remove your number. To join again, reply START or sign a new consent form.

6. Help

Reply HELP for help, or email henry@boss.technology.

7. Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Because these are drill messages, do not rely on them as your only source of information in a real emergency; follow the instructions of the official authorities.

8. Privacy

We handle your mobile number and replies as described in our Privacy Policy. We never share or sell your mobile number or consent for marketing purposes.

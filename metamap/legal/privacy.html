Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This policy explains how Boss.Technology (“we”, “us”) handles personal information in MetaMAP, including the MetaMAP Drill Alerts text-message program and the daily report sent by email.

1. Information we collect

  • Drill alerts (SMS): your mobile phone number, the messages we send you, your replies (for example SI / NO), and the time of each message.
  • Daily report (email): the email addresses of the people who receive it.
  • The MetaMAP web page does not use cookies or trackers. Settings you choose (such as language) are stored only in your own browser.

2. How we use it

Only to run drills and send the report: to deliver drill messages, record whether each participant replied and whether they reported being safe or needing help, show that status to the organization’s emergency coordinators, and deliver the report to its recipients. We do not use this information for marketing.

3. Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third party.

We use the following service providers only to operate the service, under their own confidentiality and security obligations:

  • Twilio — delivers and receives text messages;
  • Resend — delivers the report email;
  • Supabase — stores message and reply records;
  • Vercel — hosts the MetaMAP web page and its functions.

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Retention

Message and reply records are kept only as long as needed to review drills. You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time (see section 5).

5. Your choices

Reply STOP to any drill message to stop receiving messages. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to the contact below.

6. Security

Records are stored in access-restricted databases; the credentials for the messaging providers are kept on the server and never sent to the browser.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
